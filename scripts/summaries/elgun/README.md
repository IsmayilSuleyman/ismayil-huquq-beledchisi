# Elgun Jafarov's summaries, 2023–2026

The 31 "new decision" emails Elgun Jafarov sent the firm between 7 June
2023 and 18 June 2026, one draft per decision in the publisher's format
(`../draft.ts`), credited to him (`author`, `circulated_at` the email's
date). The email frame is gone; his topic line, his passages, his
underline, bold and italic, his (A)/(B)/(C) headings and the decisions'
sub-lists are kept as he wrote them, so the method's warnings about
lead-ins and citations are expected here. Two topic lines are not his:
the first email had none and the second's ran to a paragraph, so those two
were written from the method's description of the emails (STYLE.md,
Appendix A). The emails linked to the Court's old page numbers; each
draft carries the current one. His one Supreme Court email (14 May 2025)
is not here: the shelf is for Constitutional Court decisions.

Published on 31 October 2026. To lay one out again after a change to the
Word layout, delete it in the admin desk's Summaries tab and run

```bash
npm run summaries:publish -- scripts/summaries/elgun/<id>.json --no-notify
```
