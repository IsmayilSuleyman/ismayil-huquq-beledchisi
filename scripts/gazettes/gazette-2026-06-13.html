İqtisadi Kabinet / Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 9–12 June 2026

The Cabinet of Ministers issued eight Decisions and three Orders during the reporting period. The cluster of nine instruments on 9 June signals a substantive legislative session. The 12 June batch reflects continued government action on energy policy.

Decisions (Qərarlar)

Energy Efficiency Information System Framework
Establishes the legal and technical framework for a state-run energy efficiency information system, enabling data collection, monitoring, and reporting on energy consumption across sectors.
DecisionNo. 169 9 Jun 2026

Amends existing regulatory provisions governing state information systems, addressing interoperability, data governance, and electronic service delivery.
DecisionNo. 170 9 Jun 2026

Modifies the schedule or scope of services covered under Azerbaijan's mandatory health insurance scheme, potentially expanding benefits or adjusting provider obligations.
DecisionNo. 171 9 Jun 2026
General Use Railway Transport Infrastructure: Maintenance & Use Rules
Adopts comprehensive rules governing the maintenance, operation, and use of general-purpose railway infrastructure. Key sectoral regulation for rail transport.
DecisionNo. 173 9 Jun 2026
Gas Supply Regulations — Implementation & Amendments
Implements or amends regulations on natural gas supply, covering distribution norms, tariff procedures, or supplier obligations.
DecisionNo. 176 12 Jun 2026

Regulatory Focus

Renewable Energy Push: Three Cabinet instruments in a single day (Decisions 176, 177, 178 — 12 June) address land designation for renewable energy in Hacıqabul and Jabrayil. This is consistent with Azerbaijan's COP29-era commitments and the 2030 renewable energy targets. Firms advising on energy investment or land rights should monitor developments in these districts closely.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 24 / 2026

1. Renewable Energy Land Policy

Three Cabinet instruments on 12 June (Decisions 176–178) collectively expand the state's renewable energy land bank in Hacıqabul and Jabrayil districts. The Jabrayil amendment updates a 2024 decision, suggesting iterative refinement of the liberated-territories energy framework. Practitioners advising on energy project development, environmental permitting, or land acquisition in these districts should treat this as a signal of accelerating regulatory activity.

2. Railway Infrastructure Reform

Decision No. 171 introduces comprehensive rules for general-use railway infrastructure maintenance and use — a significant sectoral regulation. This may affect concession agreements, infrastructure contracts, and rail freight arrangements. Corporate and project finance teams should review existing agreements for alignment with the new rules once the full text is published on e-qanun.az.

3. Parliamentary Land Reform Package

The June 12 parliamentary session placed a wide-ranging Land Code amendment on the agenda, touching land lease, land market, privatization, and municipal land management. This is potentially the most commercially significant bill of the week. If adopted, it could alter the legal basis for a wide range of property transactions. Watch for further readings in July.

4. Digital Governance & Health Insurance

Decisions 169 (state information systems) and 168 (energy efficiency data system) together advance the government's digital infrastructure agenda. Decision 170 (mandatory health insurance) may affect employer obligations. Compliance teams in regulated sectors should monitor the full texts for operative provisions once gazetted on e-qanun.az.
