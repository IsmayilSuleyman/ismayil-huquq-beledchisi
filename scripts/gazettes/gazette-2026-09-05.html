İqtisadi Kabinet / Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 27 August – 4 September 2026

The Cabinet of Ministers issued two Decisions and thirteen Orders during the reporting period, in two clusters: a large batch of Decree-implementation Orders on 27–28 August, and a further batch on 3–4 September. Nothing was recorded between 23–26 August.

Decisions (Qərarlar)

DecisionNo. 261 28 Aug 2026
Diplomatic Service Remuneration Rules — Amendment
Amends the 2008 Cabinet Decision (No. 11) on the rules for providing benefits and compensation to diplomatic service personnel.
DecisionNo. 262 4 Sep 2026
Naming of New Baku Metro Station — "20 Sentyabr"
Formally names the new Baku Metro station "20 Sentyabr" ("20 September"), commemorating the date of the 1994 "Contract of the Century" oil agreement.

Orders (Sərəncamlar)

OrderNo. 648s 3 Sep 2026
Small & Medium Business, Investment and Export Promotion Agency — Implementation
Implements the Presidential Decree approving the charter and structure of the new Agency for the Development of Small and Medium Business, Investment and Export Promotion, formed by merging the existing SME agency (KOBİA) and the export/investment promotion agency (AZPROMO) under the Ministry of Economy. The reorganisation takes effect 1 October 2026.
OrderNo. 647s 3 Sep 2026

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 23 Aug – 4 Sep 2026

1. SME & Export-Promotion Agency Merger

Order No. 648s operationalises the merger of KOBİA and AZPROMO into a single Agency for Small and Medium Business, Investment and Export Promotion under the Ministry of Economy, effective 1 October 2026. Firms advising SME clients or exporters on state support programmes, grants, or export-promotion services should track the transition — existing agreements, registrations, and points of contact with either legacy agency may need to be re-confirmed once the merger takes effect.

2. Azerbaijan–Armenia Track Continues at SCO

The Aliyev–Pashinyan meeting on the margins of the Bishkek SCO Summit did not produce a new signed agreement but reaffirmed the initialed peace text and flagged concrete trade dividends — petroleum product exports to Armenia and third-country grain transit through Azerbaijan. Practitioners in trade, logistics, and cross-border transport should watch for implementing regulations if these arrangements are formalised.

3. Quiet Week for New Legislation

No new laws, bills, or parliamentary decrees were recorded this period — consistent with the Milli Majlis's summer recess. The last substantive legislative burst was the 14 July omnibus package (Laws 452–472-VIIQD) touching the Tax, Land, Civil, and Administrative Offenses Codes. Firms should expect renewed parliamentary activity once the autumn session opens.

4. Cultural & Heritage Sector Decree Activity

Order No. 647s (sericulture and carpet-weaving) continues a pattern of decree-level support for traditional craft and cultural-heritage sectors. Combined with the "20 Sentyabr" metro station naming (Decision No. 262), this reflects continued state attention to national heritage and historical commemoration themes.
